Parsanegar (پارسانگار) is a Microsoft Word VSTO add-in designed for Persian-language academic writing at Iranian universities. It adds a dedicated ribbon tab to Word, giving students and researchers structured document templates, Persian text correction tools, citation management, collaborative sharing, and cloud synchronization — all without leaving the familiar Word environment.

## What Parsanegar Does

Parsanegar solves the practical challenges of Persian academic writing in Microsoft Word:

- **Structured templates** — create documents pre-formatted to the exact standards of Yazd University, Islamic Azad University, and other supported institutions
- **Persian text correction** — automatically apply correct half-spaces (نیم‌فاصله), punctuation (سجاوندی), and spelling rules
- **Citation management** — insert in-text citations, import BibTeX files from Google Scholar, and switch between APA and IEEE styles
- **Cloud sync** — save and retrieve documents via the Parsanegar server; share documents with supervisors and advisors
- **Captions and cross-references** — number figures, tables, and formulas automatically and insert cross-references anywhere
- **Export workflows** — produce reviewer-ready PDFs, print-ready grayscale PDFs, and advisor-friendly unlocked Word files

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the add-in">
    Download and run the Parsanegar installer on Windows. The add-in requires Microsoft Word (Office 2016 or later) and .NET Framework 4.8. See [Installation](/installation) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an account and log in">
    Open Microsoft Word and click the **Parsanegar** tab in the ribbon. Click **ورود** (Login) and enter your mobile number to register or sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new academic document">
    In the Document Manager, select **ایجاد سند جدید** (Create New Document), choose your document type (thesis, dissertation, project) and university template, then follow the setup wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write, correct, and export">
    Use the ribbon tools to format headings, insert citations, correct Persian text, and export to PDF when your document is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Parsanegar documents must be stored in your workspace directory (default: `Documents\ShivaNegarWorkspace`). Opening a document outside this folder will disable the add-in controls.
</Note>
